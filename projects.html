Current · University of Missouri

Hybrid²: Creating Equitable Spaces for Science Discourse in Blended Learning Environments

Role: Project Manager
Principal Investigator: Kui Xie
Funding: Institute of Education Sciences

Hybrid² is a research-practice partnership focused on middle school science. The project investigates how purposeful integration of locally and culturally relevant phenomena with digital technologies can support engaging, discourse-rich learning environments.

The work brings researchers and teachers together to design, enact, study, and refine science learning experiences. A central goal is to understand how technology can support classroom discourse while preserving the importance of teacher guidance, hands-on investigation, peer interaction, and locally meaningful science phenomena.

My work on the project includes research coordination, teacher partnership and recruitment, measurement and design, classroom data collection, and analysis of data. We are working on several papers related to this project at various stages in the pipeline, stay tuned!

Dissertation program of research · Virginia Commonwealth University

Student-Centered Approaches to Analyzing Engagement and Discourse

My doctoral research developed a three-paper program focused on how students experience and participate in middle school science classrooms. Across the dissertation, I combined systematic review methods with quantitative, qualitative, and computational analyses.

I examined spoken classroom discourse using NLP, student profiles and perspectives on engagement and relevance, and small-group video discourse with attention to agency, authority, and collaboration.

Methodological development

Computational analysis of collaborative discourse

This line of work develops ways to identify interactional features such as agency, epistemic authority, and collaboration in small-group classroom discourse. The goal is to evaluate whether NLP and machine-learning approaches can help researchers navigate large classroom video corpora while retaining theoretically meaningful distinctions and qualitative interpretability.
